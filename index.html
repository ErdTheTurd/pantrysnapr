Features

Recipes from what you already have

Pantrysnapr starts from the ingredients on hand, so the recipes match what is already in your kitchen.

iPhone

Works on the iPhone Duo and regular iPhones.

Clear allergen badges

Common allergens are flagged with a badge:

  • Milk
  • Eggs
  • Fish
  • Shellfish
  • Tree nuts
  • Peanuts
  • Wheat
  • Soy
  • Sesame

Always check labels. Badges make these allergens easier to notice. They are not a substitute for reading labels on foods and ingredients.
