What it does

A short list of what is in the outline. Nothing more.

  • The photo

    Take a picture of the fridge or the pantry.

  • The ingredient list

    Review what the photo found. Add what it missed. Remove what does not belong.

  • The order

    Recipes you can make from the list come first. Then recipes missing one item. Then recipes missing two.

  • Allergen badges

    A recipe can show badges for milk, eggs, fish, shellfish, tree nuts, peanuts, wheat, soy, and sesame. Always check the label on the food itself.

  • On the device

    Photo and recipe work is designed to happen on the device.

    For the iPhone Duo and for a regular iPhone.
